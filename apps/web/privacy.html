The Funfair · Your data

Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-17 · Last updated and effective 17 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data The Funfair collects when you play, why we need it, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it. It is written with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in mind.

The short version

  • As a guest, we store nothing about you on our servers. A few game settings stay in your own browser.
  • With an account, we keep your email, username, gender, a scrambled (hashed) form of your password (or, if you continue with Google, your Google account ID instead), your tickets and the rounds you play with their statistics.
  • If you finish a Rally Trail run, your username and best time of the day can appear on its public daily leaderboard.
  • We use one cookie to keep you logged in, and two short-lived ones only while you continue with Google. No analytics, no tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • Ads are optional: only if you choose to watch one for tickets does Google load an ad, and Google may then use its own cookies (asking for your consent first where the law requires it). We don't give Google your email or username.
  • You can delete your account at any time from the account menu in the game, and ask for a copy of your data by emailing hassanhmedeh@gmail.com.

1.Who is responsible

The data controller for The Funfair is Hasan Hmedeh, who runs the game as an individual. For anything about your personal data, email hassanhmedeh@gmail.com. We have not appointed a data protection officer, as we are not required to.

The rules for using the game are in our Terms of Service.

2.What data we collect

We only collect what the game needs to work. We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number, date of birth or location, and we do not use your camera or microphone.

2.1 Account data (when you sign up)

  • Email address: to log you in and, if needed, to contact you about your account.
  • Username: 3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores, shown in the game.
  • Gender: female, male, other, or “prefer not to say”, as you choose at sign-up (accounts made before we asked are asked once, after logging in). It is not shown to other players.
  • Password (if you sign up with your email): we never store your password itself. We store only a salted hash made with scrypt, a one-way function designed to be slow to crack, which lets us check your password when you log in without being able to read it.
  • Google account ID (if you continue with Google): when you choose “Continue with Google”, Google tells us your account’s ID, your email address (and that Google has verified it) and your name. We keep the ID, to recognise you next time, and the email address as your account’s email. Your name is only used to suggest a username and is not stored. We never receive your Google password, and we ask for nothing else from your Google account. An account made this way has no password with us.
  • Account dates: when the account was created and last updated.
  • Terms acceptance: the date and time you accepted the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, and which version (for example 2026-10-09). At sign-up you also confirm you are 15 or older, or have a parent’s or guardian’s permission.
  • An internal account ID that links your data together.

2.2 Ticket data

  • Your current ticket balance and the time of your last purchase (used to work out when you can collect the next pack).
  • Your purchase history: for each pack, the date and time, quantity, price (currently always 0), currency, payment provider (currently “free”) and your balance after the purchase. Tickets are free; we collect no payment details.
  • The ads you watch for tickets: for each ad you choose to watch, when it started and, if you watched it to the end, when and how many tickets it gave you (such an ad also appears in your purchase history, with “ad” as the provider). We use this to give you the tickets once and to apply the limit on ads per day. Records of ads that were not watched to the end are deleted after an hour.
  • What you buy with tickets at the booth’s shop: for each treat or souvenir, the item, the date and time, the tickets spent and your balance after; and the souvenirs you own, when you got them and whether you’re wearing them.

2.3 Gameplay data

Each time you spend tickets on a ride or game we record a round:

  • the attraction, the number of tickets spent, and the start and end times;
  • whether the round was completed or left early;
  • the round’s statistics measured by the game in your browser, such as duration, top speed, maximum and minimum G-force, lap or race times, finishing position, altitude, distance, height or score.

From these we work out your personal bests and totals. Walking around the park, where you go and what you look at are not recorded.

When you finish a run of the Rally Trail, its time goes on that day’s leaderboard: we keep the run’s time, when you handed it in, the board day it counts for (boards run from noon to noon, Beirut time) and whether staff took it off the board. Everyone who visits the game, guests included, can see the day’s top ten usernames with their best times, on the boards in the park and in the trail’s card and screen. Earlier days’ boards are kept for our staff and are no longer shown in the game. They are deleted with your account.

2.4 Ideas you share at the Idea Box

If you leave a suggestion at the Idea Box in the park, we store:

  • what it is about (for example rides, treats or a problem), what you wrote (up to 500 characters), and when;
  • where it stands (waiting, accepted, in development, done or declined) and when that last changed, our answer if we give one, when, and the username of the member of staff who wrote it;
  • whether you have seen our latest news about it.

Our staff see your suggestion together with your username. Other players do not see it. Please don’t put personal details, such as your real name, address or phone number, in a suggestion.

2.5 Technical data

  • IP address: like every website, our servers see your IP address when your browser connects. Our game server uses it, together with a counter, to limit how many sign-up and log-in attempts can come from one address in a short time. These counters are held only in the server’s memory for about a minute and are not saved in our database.
  • Hosting logs: our hosting providers (see section 6) may automatically log requests, including IP address, date and time, the page or address requested, and browser type, to deliver the service, keep it secure and diagnose errors. We do not use these logs to build profiles of you.
  • Session token: when you log in, your browser receives a signed token containing only your account ID (see Cookies).

2.6 When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write, and use it only to answer you.

2.7 Guests

If you play without an account, we do not store anything about you in our database. Only the technical data in 2.5 and the settings stored on your own device (section 4) are involved. Guests are not offered ads.

2.8 Ads you choose to watch

When your free pack isn't ready, the game offers a short ad in exchange for tickets. Nothing about ads happens unless you click “Watch an ad”: only then does your browser load Google’s ad service, Google AdSense (H5 Games Ads), provided by Google Ireland Ltd. (and Google LLC). To choose, show and measure the ad, and to prevent ad fraud, Google receives your IP address, information about your browser and device, and the address of our page, and may read or set cookies or similar identifiers on your device. Google uses this data as an independent controller under Google’s Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

We do not give Google your email address, username or any other account data, and Google does not tell us who you are. Our game server only learns that an ad you started was watched to the end, so it can give you the tickets (see 2.2).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Google shows you a consent message before it uses cookies for ads or shows personalised ads. If you say no, Google may still show you non-personalised ads, which use cookies or similar identifiers only for purposes such as limiting how often you see an ad and fighting fraud. You can change your choice later through the privacy settings link Google shows on the page, or by clearing your browser’s site data.

6.Who else handles your data

We never sell or rent your personal data and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We use a few service providers (“processors”) that handle data on our behalf, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Vercel Inc. (United States) hosts the website itself and delivers the game’s files to your browser. It processes your IP address and request details to do so, and may keep short-lived request logs.
  • Game server (API) hosting: Vercel Inc. (USA), which runs our game server as serverless functions in its Frankfurt, Germany region. It runs the server that handles accounts, tickets and rounds, and so processes your IP address and the data you send.
  • Database hosting: Neon (provided through Vercel), in the AWS Frankfurt, Germany region (eu-central-1). It stores the account, ticket and gameplay data described in section 2.
  • Google Fonts (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC): the pages load the Lilita One and DM Sans typefaces from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. To send the font files, Google’s servers receive your IP address and basic browser information. Google says it does not use Google Fonts data to build profiles or target ads, and no Google cookies are set. See Google Fonts’ privacy FAQ and Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Sign in with Google (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC), only if you choose “Continue with Google”: you sign in on Google’s own pages, under Google’s Privacy Policy, and Google then sends us your Google account ID, email address and name (see section 2.1). Google learns that you used it to sign in to The Funfair.
  • Google AdSense (Google Ireland Ltd. / Google LLC), only if you choose to watch an ad for tickets: Google collects the data described in section 2.8 directly from your browser, as an independent controller under Google’s Privacy Policy. We do not send Google any of your account data.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it (for example to a court or the police with a valid legal request), or to protect the rights and safety of players or the service. If the game is ever taken over by someone else, your data would move to them only under this policy, and we would tell you in advance.

Apart from the optional ads in section 2.8, we do not use any analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, social media plugins, session recording or error-tracking services.

7.International transfers

Your account, ticket and gameplay data are stored and processed in Frankfurt, Germany. Vercel, Neon and Google are based in the United States, however, so your IP address, request details and, for support or maintenance, stored data may be accessed from there. Vercel and Google are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found to provide an adequate level of protection, and they also offer the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses as an additional safeguard. For Neon we rely on the safeguards in Neon’s data processing agreement, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. If any other provider processes data outside the European Economic Area, we make sure an equivalent safeguard is in place. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

9.Security

We protect your data with measures suited to a small game, including:

  • passwords stored only as salted scrypt hashes, never in readable form;
  • a session cookie that page scripts cannot read (HttpOnly), is restricted from cross-site use (SameSite=Lax), and is sent only over HTTPS in production, with a signed token that expires after 30 days;
  • encrypted connections (HTTPS) in production;
  • rate limits on sign-up and log-in attempts, and log-in checks designed not to reveal whether an email has an account;
  • every request for account data is checked so you can only see and change your own data;
  • access to servers and the database limited to the operator.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will tell you and the supervisory authority as the GDPR requires. Please use a password you do not use anywhere else.

10.Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws in other countries) you have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy of it;
  • rectification: have inaccurate data corrected;
  • erasure: have your data deleted;
  • restriction: ask us to limit how we use your data in certain cases;
  • portability: receive the data you gave us in a structured, machine-readable format and pass it on;
  • object to uses based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on consent (we currently do not rely on consent for anything);
  • set instructions for what happens to your data after your death, if you live in France.

How to use them

  • A copy of your data: email hassanhmedeh@gmail.com from the address linked to your account. We send you a file with your account details, ticket balance, every purchase, every round with its statistics, your shop orders and souvenirs, and anything you left at the Idea Box.
  • Delete account: in the same menu, choose Delete account and confirm with your password. Your account and all its tickets, purchases and rounds are deleted straight away and you are logged out.
  • Anything else (for example correcting your email or username, or if you cannot log in): email hassanhmedeh@gmail.com from the address linked to your account.

We answer requests within one month, which can be extended by two more months for complex requests (we will tell you if so). It is free. We may need to confirm your identity first, for example by asking you to write from your account email.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first so we can try to fix it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the country where you live or work or where the issue happened. In France this is the CNIL (Commission Nationale de l’Informatique et des Libertés). Elsewhere in the EU you can find your authority on the European Data Protection Board’s website.

11.Children

Accounts are for people aged 15 or older, or younger with a parent’s or guardian’s permission (15 is the age of digital consent in France, under article 8 of the GDPR and French law). We ask everyone to confirm this at sign-up, and we collect the same minimal data from every player. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 15 has created an account without your permission, contact us and we will delete it.

12.Automated decisions

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing that have legal or similarly significant effects, and we do not profile you. The game automatically applies its own rules (for example the 5-hour wait between ticket packs and the ticket cost of each ride), but these are the same for everyone.

13.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the law changes. The version date at the top always shows the current version. If we make significant changes, for example new kinds of data or new purposes, we will tell you in the game and, where appropriate, by email before they take effect. If a change requires your consent, we will ask for it.
