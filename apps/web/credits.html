The Funfair · Thank you

Credits & licences

Last updated 22 October 2026

The Funfair is built by Hasan Hmedeh, but a fair needs a crowd. The people you meet, the dogs on their leashes, the letters on the signs and the engines under the rides were made by others and shared under open licences. Here they all are, with who made them and the licence each one comes under.

The short version

  • The people are from the Microsoft Rocketbox Avatar Library (MIT) and MakeHuman (CC0), with extra moves from Quaternius (CC0).
  • The dogs are by Quaternius (CC0). The fonts are under the SIL Open Font License.
  • The 3D engine is three.js (MIT), the physics cannon-es (MIT), and the screen effects postprocessing (zlib) and N8AO (CC0).
  • Every texture is drawn by the game’s own code, and every sound is made live in your browser.
  • The full licence texts ship with the game: see the licence texts.

5.Textures & sounds

For now, every texture in the park, from the grass and the tarmac to the striped tents, is drawn by the game’s own code as it loads, and every sound, from the coasters to the jet engines, is synthesised live in your browser with the Web Audio API. There are no third-party pictures or recordings to credit yet.

Real-world materials Coming soon

The park is getting real wood, metal, stone and canvas materials, shared under CC0. Their makers will be listed here when they arrive.

Crowd sounds Coming soon

The crowd’s murmur, laughter and cheers will come from CC0 recordings, credited here when they arrive.
